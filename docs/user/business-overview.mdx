# Business Overview

The Business Overview page brings together the main information HMRC holds for one of your businesses and the tax tasks that relate to it.

To open it, go to your account and select the business you want to view. If you have more than one business, choose each one separately to see its own details and obligations.

## Business details

The **Business Details** card shows the key information for the selected business, including:

- Business ID
- Trading name
- Business and trade type
- Date the business started
- Business address

Select **Show More** to see extra information when it is available, such as the date the business ceased trading and your quarterly reporting-period choice. Select **Show Less** to hide these extra details again.

If no details are shown, check that you have entered the correct National Insurance number on your account page and that your HMRC account is linked.

## Your obligations

The **Obligations** card shows the quarterly and year-end tasks HMRC has recorded for the selected business.

1. Choose a tax year from the drop-down list.
2. Review the quarterly and year-end sections below it.
3. Each item shows its reporting period and due date.

An obligation may be shown as:

- **Fulfilled** — HMRC has received it. Where available, the received date is also shown.
- **Open** — it still needs your attention. Use the button next to it to open the relevant quarterly update or year-end tax return page.

The buttons take you to the relevant part of the app with the business and reporting-period details included. Submitting quarterly updates and year-end tax returns is still being developed.

## Tips

- Check the tax year before reviewing your obligations; a different year can have different tasks and due dates.
- Use the Business Overview page to confirm you are working on the right business before you start an update or tax return.
- If something looks incorrect, check it against your HMRC records before taking action.
