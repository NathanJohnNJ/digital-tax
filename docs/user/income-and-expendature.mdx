# Income and Expenditure

The Income and Expenditure page helps you add up your business income and expenses for a reporting period. It gives you a simple total to use when preparing a quarterly update or year-end tax return.

This page is a calculator. It does not send figures to HMRC or submit a tax return.

## Entering totals directly

On the right of the page, enter:

- **Total Income** — the total money your business received.
- **Total Expenditure** — the total business expenses you paid.

The **Total** field updates automatically. It is your income minus your expenditure.

Use this option when you have already added up your records elsewhere.

## Using the calculators

If you still need to add up individual amounts, select **Show calculators**.

You will see separate **Income** and **Expenses** calculators.

1. Enter an amount in the first row.
2. Select the **plus (+)** button to add another row.
3. Keep adding amounts until all of your income or expenses are included.
4. Check the total at the bottom of each calculator.

Each calculator total is automatically copied into the Total Income or Total Expenditure field. The overall Total then updates automatically.

To remove an amount you no longer need, select the **minus (−)** button next to that row.

## Before you use your total

- Enter amounts in pounds and pence, for example `125.50`.
- Only include figures for the reporting period you are preparing.
- Keep your receipts, invoices and other business records so you can check the amounts later.
- Review the income and expenditure totals before using them in a quarterly update or year-end tax return.

Your figures are kept in the calculator while you are on the page. If you leave or refresh the page, enter them again from your records.
