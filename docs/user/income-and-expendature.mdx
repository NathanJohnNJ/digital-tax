## Income and Expenditure

<br></br>

<center>Use this page to total business income and expenses for a period. The calculator is a preparation aid only: it does not save your figures or send them to HMRC.</center>

<br></br>

---

### Enter totals directly

Enter figures in **Total Income** and **Total Expenditure** if you have already added up your records. The **Total** field updates to show income minus expenditure.

Use pounds and pence, for example `125.50`. Enter amounts as positive numbers; the calculator subtracts expenditure for you.

<br></br>

---

### Use the calculators

Select **Show calculators** to open the detailed tables. The page separates income, expenses, and disallowable expenses into their own tables. Expand a table to review its categories and enter an amount for each category you use.

The **plus** control adds an available category to its table, and the **minus** control removes a category. Totals for income and expenses are copied into the direct-entry fields, and the overall **Total** updates automatically. Disallowable expenses are shown separately; check the HMRC guidance for how they apply to your records.

<br></br>

---

### Use figures in an update

Select **Use figures in quarterly update** to carry your figures to the quarterly form. This button stays disabled until at least one amount has been entered. If the calculators are visible and you have edited a calculator amount, income and all expense categories are transferred as itemised figures. Otherwise, the three totals in the main table are transferred as consolidated figures.

The quarterly form is still tied to a business and reporting period. If those are not already selected, choose an open quarterly obligation before submitting. Transferred figures are held in this browser tab for the hand-off and are removed after the quarterly form reads them. Values on the income page otherwise exist only in its current state; leaving or refreshing that page clears them.
