## Linking Your HMRC Account

<br></br>

<center>Linking your HMRC account allows the app to submit your tax information securely on your behalf.
This process uses HMRC’s official OAuth system and is fully authorised.</center>
<br></br>

---

### Step 1 — Start the Link Process

Inside the app, go to:

**Account → Login**

Login to Digital Tax or register for a new account.

The login/registration is handled by Auth0 so your password is never shared with Digital Tax.

Once logged in, you will be redirected back to your account within Digital Tax.

### Step 2 — Start the Link Process

Inside your account, click the '**Link HMRC Account**' button.

You will be redirected to HMRC’s secure login page.

### Step 3 — Sign In With HMRC

You will sign in using your Government Gateway credentials.

HMRC will ask you to confirm that you want to grant permission to this app.  
You are not sharing your password — HMRC handles the login.

### Step 4 — Grant Permissions

HMRC will ask you to approve access for:

- Making Tax Digital (MTD) VAT  
- MTD Income Tax (ITSA)  
- Reading and submitting tax information  

You can revoke this at any time.

### Step 5 — Return to the App

Once approved, HMRC redirects you back to your account page.

You should now see:

<br></br>
![Successfully connected with HMRC account](http://localhost:3000/images/connected.png)
<br></br>
---

### Troubleshooting

- **Incorrect Government Gateway password**  
  HMRC will show an error — simply retry.

- **Expired session**  
  Restart the linking process.

- **Permissions not granted**  
  You may need to re‑link your account.
