## Linking Your HMRC Account

<br></br>

<center>Linking your HMRC account gives Digital Tax permission to access supported tax information on your behalf through HMRC OAuth.</center>

<br></br>

---

### Step 1 — Sign in to Digital Tax

Go to **Account → Login** and sign in or register. Auth0 handles your Digital Tax sign-in; your password is not shared with Digital Tax.

### Step 2 — Start the HMRC link

From your account page, select **Link HMRC Account**. You will be redirected to HMRC's secure authorization page.

### Step 3 — Sign in with HMRC

Sign in using your Government Gateway credentials. HMRC handles that sign-in and will ask you to approve the requested access.

### Step 4 — Approve access

The current integration requests permission to read and submit supported Self Assessment information for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. It does not request MTD VAT access.

### Step 5 — Return to Digital Tax

After approval, HMRC redirects you to your account page. Your HMRC connection is stored with your Digital Tax account, so you do not need to link it again at every login and can use it from another device when signed in to the same account.

HMRC may require you to authorize again if the refresh authorization is no longer valid. If that happens, select **Link HMRC Account** and repeat the steps.

<br></br>

---

### Troubleshooting

- **HMRC sign-in or permission error:** follow HMRC's instructions, then restart the linking process.
- **Authorization expired or revoked:** sign in to Digital Tax and link HMRC again.
- **HMRC link does not complete:** make sure you return to the same browser session where you started the process.

The integration currently uses HMRC Sandbox endpoints. A successful Sandbox connection is not a live HMRC filing.
