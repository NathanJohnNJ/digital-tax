## Year-End Tax Return

<br></br>

<center>The year-end page currently retrieves and saves the self-employment annual summary for a selected business and tax year. It is not a complete Self Assessment return and does not make a final declaration.</center>

<br></br>

---

### Open the annual summary

From the selected business's **Obligations** list, choose an open year-end obligation. The page uses the business and period in that link to identify the tax year. If the page reports that required details are missing, return to the obligations list and open the task there.

The app may retrieve an existing annual summary from HMRC's sandbox and prefill the fields it recognises.

<br></br>

---

### Review adjustments and allowances

Enter only the adjustments and allowances that apply to the business and tax year. The form includes accounting and basis adjustments, overlap relief, balancing charges, own use of goods or services, and several capital and trading allowances. It also asks whether business details changed recently and provides a Class 4 National Insurance exemption reason field.

The form saves the self-employment annual summary only. Other income sources, claims or reliefs, the final tax calculation, and the declaration are outside this page. Check amounts and eligibility against your records and current HMRC guidance before saving.

<br></br>

---

### Save and understand the result

Select **Save annual summary with HMRC** to send the summary to the HMRC sandbox. A success message means the sandbox accepted the request; it does not file a complete live tax return or submit a final declaration.

This feature is under active development. Do not use this app to make a live tax filing or rely on it for tax advice.
